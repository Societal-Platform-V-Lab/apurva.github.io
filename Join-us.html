Head – Operations & Delivery Excellence

Apurva.ai | Bangalore | Full-Time

Position Overview:

We are seeking an experienced, entrepreneurial Operations & Delivery Excellence Head with a strong product and growth mindset to drive operational excellence across internal and external initiatives. This role requires a strategic thinker who can translate vision into action, combining SaaS-driven growth thinking with hands-on execution capabilities. We're looking for someone who approaches operations from a product-first perspective, focusing on scalability, user experience, and continuous improvement rather than traditional project implementation. The ideal candidate will have 10-12 years of experience in managing project/program/delivery operations and will oversee the organisation's day-to-day operations bringing in the rigour of execution while fostering a culture of operational excellence and collaborative innovation both within the organisation and with external clients.

Key Responsibilities:

Strategic Project Execution:

  • Develop comprehensive project roadmaps while maintaining flexibility to adapt to changing needs
  • Drive projects from conception to successful completion, ensuring alignment with strategic objectives
  • Create and maintain detailed project and action plans with clear timelines and deliverables
  • Plan and execute multiple projects including field operations, physical/digital/hybrid events
  • Ensure there is executional rigour in all elements of Apurva.ai's lifecycle.

Team operations execution:

  • Lead the day-to-day operations of the organisation, and be accountable for seeing the actions items through
  • Work closely with cross-functional teams to ensure seamless collaboration
  • Ensure all team activities comply with org policies and procedures
  • Work with the respective org teams to ensure seamless process of onboarding client partners and vendor partners
  • Procure, monitor, maintain the digital tools required for the team

Process Excellence:

  • Design and implement scalable operational processes that drive efficiency while maintaining quality
  • Lead continuous improvement initiatives across all operational touch points
  • Establish and maintain digital infrastructure to support team operations
  • Monitor and optimise tool usage and effectiveness
  • Implement and maintain operational tracking systems
  • Continuously analyse and improve delivery workflows to reduce costs and enhance quality

Performance & Analytics:

  • Develop and track meaningful KPIs that align with organisational objectives
  • Transform data insights into actionable operational improvements
  • Create and maintain reporting frameworks that drive accountability and transparency
  • Monitor and report on team performance metrics
  • Maintain detailed documentation of operational processes and outcomes
  • Evaluate team performance against established targets

Stakeholder Management:

  • Build and nurture strategic relationships with internal teams and external partners
  • Drive collaborative problem-solving across diverse stakeholder groups
  • Facilitate effective communication and alignment between all parties
  • Act as a key liaison between internal teams, client partners, and vendors

Requirements:

  • Outcome driven, proactive, go-getter with strong execution rigour
  • 10-12 years of proven experience in managing product/program/service delivery operations, preferably in SaaS environments
  • Strong product and growth mindset with demonstrated ability to think beyond project implementation
  • Experience in scaling operations in high-growth environments
  • Track record of implementing data-driven, product-centric operational solutions
  • Preferably from development sector background
  • Strong expertise in operational tools and systems management
  • Demonstrated ability to manage both strategic initiatives and day-to-day tactical operations
  • Experience in implementing and optimising digital tools and processes
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication and problem-solving skills
  • Proven ability to collaborate with diverse stakeholder groups
  • Experience in process optimization and business operations management
  • Proficiency in CRM applications/operations management software is an added advantage

Essential Qualities:

  • Strategic yet hands-on leader who can both architect and execute operational workflows
  • Creative and flexible approach to problem-solving
  • Natural collaborator able to work effectively with internal teams and external organisations
  • Strong ability to see the bigger picture while managing detailed execution
  • Excellent at building consensus and driving discussions across stakeholder groups
  • Comfortable moving between strategic planning and tactical execution
  • Detail-oriented with strong process management skills
  • Product-first mindset with ability to see operations through a growth lens
  • Experience in translating product thinking into operational excellence
  • Ability to balance user experience with operational efficiency
  • Growth-oriented approach to problem-solving and process optimisation
