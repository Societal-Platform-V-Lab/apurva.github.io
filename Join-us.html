Lead – Digital Marketing & Content

Apurva.ai | Bangalore | Full-Time

Position Overview:

Apurva.ai is seeking a dynamic Digital Marketing & Content Lead to drive our brand presence, engagement and impact. This role blends strategic marketing with compelling content creation to enhance our outreach, grow our user base, and establish Apurva.ai as a thought leader in the development sector. The ideal candidate is a creative storyteller, data-driven marketer and purpose-driven professional passionate about driving social change through technology.

Key Responsibilities:

1. Digital Marketing Strategy & Execution

  • Develop and implement a comprehensive digital marketing strategy aligned with organizational goals.
  • Plan and execute multi-channel campaigns leveraging organic and paid strategies (Google Ads, social media ads, etc.).
  • Conduct A/B testing and leverage analytics to refine campaign performance.

2. Social Media & Community Engagement

  • Own and manage the organization’s presence across social platforms (LinkedIn)
  • Plan and execute content calendars, campaigns, and engagement activities.
  • Foster user engagement and advocacy through interactive content and conversations.

3. Content Strategy & Creation

  • Develop a content strategy that aligns with Apurva.ai’s mission, engages users, and drives awareness.
  • Create compelling, research-backed content across various formats (blogs, case studies, whitepapers, videos, newsletters, infographics, etc.).
  • Optimize content for SEO to drive inbound traffic and organic reach.
  • Collaborate with internal teams and users to uncover meaningful narratives and success stories.

4. Branding & Design

  • Ensure all marketing materials align with Apurva.ai’s brand voice and values.
  • Design engaging visuals for social media, website assets, and promotional campaigns.
  • Engage and coordinate with external vendors and partners for PR, website management, and creative assets.

5. Analytics & Optimisation

  • Track website traffic, conversion rates, and content performance using tools like Google Analytics and HubSpot.
  • Prepare performance reports with actionable insights to refine marketing strategies.
  • Continuously improve content and campaigns based on data-driven findings.

Qualifications:

  • Experience: 8-10 years in digital marketing and content strategy, preferably in SaaS organizations.
  • Expertise: in social media strategy, content creation, SEO, and campaign management.
  • Tools: Proficiency in Canva, Adobe Suite, HubSpot, Google Analytics, SEMrush, and social media scheduling tools.
  • Creative Skills: Exceptional storytelling, design sensibilities, and ability to craft compelling narratives.
  • Data-Driven: Strong analytical skills to measure success, identify gaps, and optimize marketing efforts.
  • Collaboration: Experience working cross-functionally with internal teams and external stakeholders.
  • Sector Knowledge: Familiarity with challenges and trends in the development sector is a strong plus.

Preferred Attributes:

  • Passion for driving social impact through digital engagement.
  • Experience launching digital campaigns for SaaS products targeting development sector audiences.
  • Exceptional communication and organizational skills.
