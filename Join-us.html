Lead – Client Success

Apurva.ai | Bangalore | Full-Time

Position Overview:

We are seeking an experienced Client Success Lead with 8–10 years in customer success roles to drive client engagement, align goals, validate use cases, ensure seamless onboarding, and focus on adoption and scaling, with a hands-on, execution-driven approach to reimagining value and ensuring successful go-lives.

Key Responsibilities:

  • Alignment & Value Exchange: Align with clients on vision and use cases, drive seamless onboarding and problem resolution, collaborate across teams, communicate with leadership, and focus on scalability, data-driven results, and continuous feedback to enhance the client experience
  • Adoption & Engagement: Collaborate closely with client partners to reimagine problem statements, drive innovation, explore Apurva’s full potential, create sustained value, and maintain strong relationships as their primary point of contact for engagement initiatives
  • Experience & Case studies: Develop customer journey maps, co-create case studies, document real impact stories, and share insights through blogs and presentations to inspire ongoing engagement and knowledge sharing across organizations
  • Performance metrics and execution: Enhance account performance by defining, executing, and analysing KPIs to ensure consistent adoption, engagement, impact, and achievement of scale targets
  • Stakeholder collaboration: Collaborate with internal teams and facilitate partnerships to ensure smooth delivery, maximize value creation, and foster meaningful connections and interactions

Requirements:

  • People-oriented, data-driven, proactive with strong execution rigor
  • 8–10 years of experience in customer/client engagement or success, preferably with digital products/platforms
  • Experience in the development sector is a plus
  • Excellent interpersonal, communication, and problem-solving skills
  • Proven ability to collaborate with diverse stakeholders and influence at all levels
  • Familiarity with CRM systems and customer engagement/analytics tools is a plus
