Lead – Content

Apurva.ai | Bangalore | Full-Time

Position Overview:

We are seeking a passionate and strategic Lead - Content to drive Apurva.ai’s content strategy and execution. In this role, you will be responsible for crafting meaningful, impactful, and diverse content to engage both existing and potential users. You will play a pivotal role in shaping how Apurva.ai communicates its value proposition to stakeholders in the development sector, ensuring that our voice is authentic, inspiring, and aligned with our mission.

Key Responsibilities:

1. Content Strategy

  • Develop and execute a comprehensive content strategy that resonates with the right audience in the development sector and drives engagement.
  • Align content with organization objectives, user needs, and market trends.
  • Innovate and experiment with content formats, including blogs, case studies, whitepapers, videos, infographics, newsletters, and more.

2. Content Creation

  • Create compelling, research-backed content that highlights Apurva.ai’s unique capabilities, success stories, and insights.
  • Collaborate with internal teams and customers to uncover meaningful narratives and use cases.
  • Ensure all content maintains a high standard of quality, relevance, and consistency in tone and messaging.

3. User Engagement

  • Develop content that strengthens relationships with existing users, fostering loyalty and advocacy.
  • Tailor content for potential users to drive awareness, acquisition, and conversions.
  • Design resources (e.g., tutorials, guides) to help users maximize the value of Apurva.ai.

4. Contribution to Thought Leadership

  • Contribute to opinion pieces, webinars, speaking engagements, and other forms of outreach initiatives to establish Apurva.ai as a thought leader in the sector.

5. Data-Driven Feedback Looping

  • Use analytics tools to track content performance, and derive insights for improvement.
  • Continuously refine content based on data-driven findings and user feedback.

6. Team Collaboration

  • Work closely with marketing, product, and design teams to align content with broader campaigns and brand goals

Qualifications:

  • Experience: 8-10 years of content creation and strategy experience, preferably in SaaS based organizations
  • Storytelling::Strong storytelling skills with the ability to translate complex ideas into accessible, engaging narratives
  • Content Expertise: Proven expertise in creating diverse formats of content (written, visual, interactive)
  • SEO: Solid understanding of SEO best practices and content optimization
  • Analytics: Proficiency in tools like Google Analytics, SEMrush, or similar for measuring content impact
  • Sector Knowledge: Familiarity with the development sector's challenges, trends, and audiences is a strong plus

Preferred Attributes:

  • A passion for creating impactful content that drives social change
  • Demonstrated success in developing campaigns or content that engage both technical and non-technical audiences
  • Exceptional communication and organizational skills

Lead – Digital Marketing

Apurva.ai | Bangalore | Full-Time

Position Overview:

We at Apurva.ai are looking for a creative and data-driven Lead - Digital Marketing to spearhead our digital marketing initiatives. This role requires a dynamic professional who combines strategic thinking, exceptional storytelling, and a passion for driving meaningful social impact. You will take ownership of developing and executing marketing strategies that amplify our brand, grow our user base, and engage our community of customers across the development sector.

Key Responsibilities:

1. Digital Marketing Strategy

Develop and implement a comprehensive digital marketing strategy aligned with organizational goals

  • Define and track KPIs to evaluate campaign effectiveness and optimize efforts
  • Identify emerging digital trends and opportunities to keep the brand relevant and at the forefront

2. Social Media Management

Own and manage the organization’s presence across key social platforms (LinkedIn, Twitter, Instagram, etc.)

  • Plan and execute content calendars, campaigns, and engagement activities to drive awareness and conversions
  • Monitor trends in the social sector and SaaS industry to craft relevant, impactful messaging

3. Content Creation and Strategy

Identify compelling content across formats – blogs, infographics, whitepapers, videos, case studies, and newsletters, and help develop byte sized content for social media and other marketing channels

  • Collaborate with internal teams and the network of users to identify and tell authentic success stories
  • Optimize content for SEO and drive inbound traffic through organic efforts

4. Design and Branding

Create visually appealing marketing materials, ensuring alignment with the brand's voice and values

  • Create engaging social media graphics, website assets, and promotional materials
  • Engage and work with vendors and partners for PR, Website etc.

5. Campaign Execution

Plan and execute multi-channel campaigns to acquire, engage, and retain customers

  • Leverage paid advertising (Google Ads, social media ads) and organic strategies for lead generation
  • Use A/B testing and data analytics to refine campaign performance

6. Analytics and Reporting

Monitor website traffic, conversion rates, and campaign metrics using tools like Google Analytics, HubSpot, and others

  • Prepare and present performance reports with actionable insights
  • Continuously iterate and improve strategies based on data-driven findings

Qualifications:

  • Experience: 8-10 years of digital marketing experience, preferably in SaaS based organizations
  • Expertise: Proven expertise in social media strategy, content creation, and marketing design
  • Tools: Proficiency with tools like Canva, Adobe Suite, HubSpot, Google Analytics, and social media scheduling platforms
  • SEO/SEM: Strong knowledge of SEO best practices, keyword research, and SEM campaigns
  • Creative Skills: Exceptional storytelling and design sensibilities to craft compelling narratives and visuals
  • Data-Driven: Strong analytical skills to measure success, identify gaps, and optimize efforts
  • Team Collaboration: Experience working cross-functionally with the rest of the functional teams both internal and external
  • Sector Knowledge: Familiarity with the development sector's challenges, trends, and audiences is a strong plus

Preferred Attributes

  • A passion for driving social change and working with purpose-driven organizations
  • Experience in launching digital campaigns for SaaS products targeting the right personas across the development sector
  • Exceptional communication and ability to efficiently collaborate with stakeholders
